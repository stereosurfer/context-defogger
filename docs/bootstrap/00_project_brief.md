# Project Brief

Status: active target brief.

Context Defogger is a public Codex skill for extracting useful thinking context
from messy AI-user conversations.

## Mission

Turn conversation logs into article embryos that explain:

- the real question behind a work milestone,
- the user's intent and later corrections,
- the AI proposals and assumptions,
- the reason proposals were accepted, modified, or rejected,
- the retained design decisions and lessons.

## Non-Goals

- No transcript export.
- No Cloudflare or static blog publishing in the initial version.
- No HTML renderer in the initial version.
- No private conversation corpus.
- No package-manager publication in this work unit.
